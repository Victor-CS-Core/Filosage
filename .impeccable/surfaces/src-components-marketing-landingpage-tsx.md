# Public landing page

- Scope and mode: public home marketing surface; Persuade.
- Audience and job: product, data, analytics, engineering, and adjacent professionals with a current 2-8 week work outcome. Help them understand the product, inspect a real course, and begin a qualified learner account.
- Action and proof: primary action explores published course outcomes; secondary action opens the existing legal-aware account flow. Proof comes from real public course metadata, the outcome-to-evidence learning runway, honest evidence states, visible access boundaries, and source-status language.
- Chosen direction: approved B hybrid. The first viewport pairs the outcome-led promise with a large real public course spread. A continuous runway explains outcome, route, practice, transfer, and evidence. A later navy evidence dossier distinguishes self-report, observed practice, and pending assessment.

Do not imply universal source verification, credentials, market outcomes, open paid checkout, or anonymous lesson bodies. Billing remains disabled. - Implementation fidelity: editorial split hero; rules and open bands rather than generic card grids; 7-15px corners; restrained soft depth; Source Serif page titles and Inter interface/body; mobile stacks course cover and outline and converts the runway to a vertical sequence.
