## 2. Information we collect

- **Account information:** Microsoft Entra External ID or Google provider identifier, verified email address, display name, profile image when supplied, and a pseudonymous identity-link record used to keep one FiloSage learning account across approved sign-in methods. Filosage does not receive the one-time code.
- **Launch-update information:** the email address and consent record you submit when joining a plan waitlist or requesting product updates.
- **Learning information:** course enrollments and creations, active-lesson and transfer responses submitted as completion evidence, lesson progress, quiz results, confidence ratings, review dates, bookmarks, notes, goals, preferences, and badges. Unfinished activity drafts are stored in your browser until completion or local-site data is cleared.
- **AI feature content:** course requests, author-provided reference labels, links and notes, tutor messages, lesson context, generated responses, token usage, request identifiers, and safety-review results. Blocked-content records contain categories and a one-way fingerprint rather than the blocked text.
- **Support, report, and operational case information:** support or content-report details, linked account or course identifiers, evidence references, internal review notes, approval decisions, and bounded audit metadata used to investigate and resolve operational matters.
- **Product and traffic information:** aggregate page views and product events such as starting a course, beginning or completing a lesson, completing practice, or demonstrating a criterion. Events may include randomly generated first-party actor and session identifiers, a pseudonymous referral code, broad route, campaign labels, acquisition channel, referring hostname, and content or experiment version. Filosage does not store the full referring page address or private learning text in product analytics.
- **Technical information:** browser and device information, IP address and security or hosting logs collected by our infrastructure providers, approximate region, error data, and consent records.
- **Payment information:** for an eligible United States paid-plan purchaser age 18 or older, Stripe handles complete card details. Filosage receives billing contact, billing address and country, customer and subscription identifiers, transaction and refund status, and limited payment metadata rather than complete card numbers. (Note: paid checkout is currently unavailable pre-launch; see section 1.)

## 3. How we use information

We use information to authenticate users; provide, synchronize, personalize, and improve learning features; evaluate product demand and learning outcomes; send updates people explicitly request; attribute broad acquisition channels; generate requested AI content; calculate progress and limits; prevent fraud and abuse; secure and troubleshoot the service; communicate material account or subscription information; comply with law; and enforce our agreements. Owner activity is excluded from first-party product measurement where the signed-in owner account is known. We do not sell personal information or use third-party behavioral advertising. We do not use your Google profile to expose the owner's private sign-in identity.

## 4. Legal bases

Where the GDPR or similar law applies, we process information to perform our contract with you, pursue legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, comply with legal obligations, and, where required, based on consent. You may withdraw consent where consent is the basis, without affecting earlier lawful processing.

## 5. Service providers and disclosures

Information may be processed by Microsoft Azure, including Microsoft Entra External ID, for customer identity, email one-time-code delivery and verification, application hosting, database, private object storage, secrets, logs, and monitoring; Google when you choose federated Google sign-in; OpenAI for requested AI features and safety controls; network and email providers for delivery and security; professional advisers and authorities where legally required; and Stripe for subscription checkout, billing management, and payment processing. Providers may only use information for contracted services, their documented independent purposes, or as law requires. We may transfer information in a merger, financing, reorganization, or sale subject to appropriate notice and safeguards.

## 6. AI data handling and transparency

Prompts, lesson context, and generated output sent through AI features are transmitted to OpenAI. Filosage requests that Responses API application state not be stored, and OpenAI states that API data is not used to train its models by default unless the customer opts in. OpenAI may retain abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days by default, or longer where legally required. Filosage identifies direct AI interactions and AI-assisted course or lesson material in the interface and machine-readable page markup. We also apply input limits, moderation, per-account quotas, and pseudonymous safety identifiers to reduce abuse and unnecessary disclosure. Do not enter secrets, highly sensitive personal information, or information you lack permission to use.

## 7. Cookies and local storage

Filosage uses essential browser storage to remember your theme and support authentication and session continuity. Optional first-party analytics storage is off until you choose **Allow analytics**. If allowed, it may keep a first-touch acquisition record, randomly generated actor and session identifiers, and duplicate-event markers used to calculate aggregate funnels. The acquisition record contains a broad channel, optional pseudonymous referral code, campaign labels, referring hostname, and landing path rather than a cross-site advertising identifier or full referring URL. Refusing or later withdrawing permission in the Privacy Center removes those optional browser identifiers and stops new optional product events. We do not currently use advertising cookies. If optional third-party analytics or marketing technologies are introduced, this notice and any required consent controls will be updated first.

## 8. Retention

Account and learning information is generally kept while your account is active. Waitlist contact information is used while requested updates remain relevant or until consent is withdrawn. After withdrawal, Filosage may retain a limited suppression and consent-history record so the address is not contacted again without a new request. Product events are retained long enough to evaluate acquisition, activation, learning, and retention trends, then deleted or de-identified when no longer reasonably needed. When you request deletion of an eligible account, Filosage closes it to new writes and records a resumable deletion request. Unconfirmed subscription cancellation, incomplete inventory, uncertain asset ownership, or interrupted work remains pending; a request is not described as complete while these checks remain unresolved. Limited legal-acceptance, completed billing-consent, payment-processor, safety, report, and enforcement records may be retained only as needed to comply with law, resolve disputes, document consent, account for transactions, or prevent abuse. AI usage and idempotency records are otherwise kept as needed to enforce limits and investigate failures. Billing records, after paid plans launch, may be retained for applicable tax, accounting, and consumer-protection periods. Hosting, authentication, and security providers may retain logs or backups under their policies.

Expired or consumed identity-link intents become eligible for pruning after 30 days. Removal currently requires an approved identity-maintenance run; no automatic pruning schedule is enabled. Authentication security events use bounded categories and pseudonymous actor keys under the current 30-day Azure Log Analytics retention. Immutable account-generation tombstones and deletion jobs remain to prevent old sessions and delayed writes from recreating deleted data and to support privacy recovery. Detailed retention schedules are pending the owner's privacy review and will be published here when complete. Until then, the practices described above apply: account and learning data are kept while an account is active; product events are deleted or de-identified when no longer reasonably needed; and legal, billing, safety, and enforcement records are kept only as long as required by law or legitimate need. Automated removal of active data is reported separately from complete erasure; the app does not claim every record was erased. Shared course-banner assets and other learners' work are retained under their ownership policy; exclusively owned assets are removed only after upload outcomes and surviving references are verified. Identity links remain while needed to keep approved sign-in methods attached to the same account. Identity-link deletion is not part of automated learning-data deletion because removing a mapping can disconnect account recovery; a separately verified identity request and reviewed deletion process are required.

## 9. Security

We use HTTPS, verified authentication tokens, server-side authorization, restricted database rules, bounded inputs, content moderation, quotas, and access-limited service credentials. No online system is perfectly secure. Protect your chosen email or Google account, use a secure device, never share a one-time code, and report suspected misuse promptly.

## 10. Your choices and rights

You can browse published topics and course outlines without an account, change your theme, and control basic site preferences. Optional first-party analytics remain off unless you allow them and can be turned off again at any time. A free account is required before lesson content or account-linked learning records are delivered. Signed-in users can export a copy of their account data, withdraw launch-update consent, control optional analytics, or request account deletion in the Privacy Center. The owner account requires a verified manual transfer or service-shutdown process before deletion so published-course control is not orphaned. Depending on where you live, you may also request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection; appeal a denied privacy request; or withdraw consent. You may have rights to know categories and recipients or to opt out of sale, sharing, targeted advertising, or certain profiling. Filosage does not currently sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. Send manual requests from the email associated with your account to legal@filosage.com so we can verify them. You may use an authorized agent where law permits. We will not discriminate against you for exercising a privacy right.

## 11. International processing

Filosage and its providers may process information in the United States and other countries that may have different data-protection laws. Where required, we rely on appropriate contractual or legal transfer safeguards.

## 12. Children and paid-plan eligibility

Filosage is a general-audience service and accounts are not intended for children under 13. Registration requires confirmation that the person creating the account is at least 13 and, if the person is a minor where they live, that a parent or legal guardian has reviewed and agreed to the legal terms. Paid subscriptions are not offered to users under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 provided personal information, contact legal@filosage.com so we can investigate and delete it where required.

## 13. Changes and complaints

We will post updates here and change the effective date. Material changes may also be shown in the service or sent by email. You may contact us with a concern and, where applicable, complain to your local data-protection authority or state regulator.

## 14. AI-assisted operational review

The owner may request an AI-assisted draft to classify or summarize a support, report, billing, legal-intake, or product-feedback matter. For those requests, Filosage sends OpenAI a bounded operational summary, recorded facts and unverified claims after basic credential and contact redaction, and approved knowledge references. Linked account-identifier fields, reporter excerpts, internal notes, authentication data, and payment credentials are excluded from the model prompt. The resulting draft is for owner review only and cannot send a message or perform an account, content, privacy, legal, or billing action. Learner-visible support requests and public replies are included in the automated account-data export. Operational owner drafts and internal notes are not included in the automated learner export; send a manual access request to legal@filosage.com if you need information beyond that export. Operational records may be retained with the underlying report or safety record as described above.
