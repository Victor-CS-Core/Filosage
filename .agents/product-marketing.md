# Product Marketing Context

**Document version:** v1
**Last updated:** 2026-08-16
**Authority:** Owner-approved product positioning, reconciled with the repository capability audit. Runtime entitlement, feature-flag, legal, privacy, and billing code remains authoritative for availability.

## Product Overview

**One-liner:** Filosage is a self-directed learning and course-creation workspace that turns a meaningful goal into a focused course, deliberate practice, useful review, and evidence the learner can inspect.

**What it does:** Visitors can inspect the structure and source state of published courses before joining. Verified learners can open lessons and keep progress, notes, practice, review timing, and evidence connected; eligible Plus, Pro, and owner accounts can create private courses in a requested language or bilingual pairing. Pro adds advanced capstone analysis, portable evidence export and revocable sharing, and course publishing, subject to the current entitlement and review rules.

**Product category:** Self-directed learning and course-creation workspace.
**Product type:** Individual web application; not a school service, LMS, team workspace, accredited program, or credential issuer.
**Business model:** Free, Plus, and Pro individual memberships. Paid checkout is a separately controlled capability and must be described as closed whenever the published billing status is closed.

## Target Audience

**Product audience:** Eligible independent students aged 13 or older, self-directed learners, career changers, working professionals, and individual course creators using Filosage primarily for their own learning.

**Initial validation cohorts:** Campaigns and interviews may focus on product, data, analytics, engineering, and adjacent knowledge workers, or another bounded learning situation. A campaign cohort is not the product eligibility boundary.

**Primary use case:** Turn a concrete learning goal into a focused route, complete deliberate practice, return when review is useful, and inspect what the record supports.

**Jobs to be done:**

- Prepare for coursework, an exam, or independent study without stitching together disconnected resources.
- Learn enough to complete a personal, creative, civic, or technical project.
- Build or demonstrate a capability for a portfolio, interview, role transition, or career goal.
- Learn a difficult concept needed for a current work decision, project, or promotion.
- Create a private course when the published library does not cover the goal.

**Secondary audience:** Individuals creating and publishing courses. Multi-author collaboration, classrooms, teams, and institutional administration are not supported.

## Problems & Pain Points

**Core problem:** A meaningful learning goal is often spread across generic chat, videos, articles, marketplace courses, improvised notes, and disconnected practice. The learner cannot easily tell what to do next, what is supported by sources, what has actually been practiced, or what the final record proves.

**Why alternatives fall short:**

- Generic chat can explain but does not inherently preserve a bounded curriculum, return schedule, evidence states, or source-status contract.
- Tutorials and videos are easy to collect but difficult to sequence around one observable outcome.
- Broad course marketplaces may not fit the learner's precise goal, time, prior knowledge, or requested course language.
- Self-made notes and flashcards require the learner to design and maintain the whole system.
- Doing nothing delays the goal and keeps uncertainty intact.

**Emotional tension:** The learner wants momentum without hype, a useful path without false certainty, and evidence without pretending that activity equals mastery.

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators:**

- Outcome and evidence are defined before the course is treated as successful.
- The Capability Cycle connects explanation, prediction or recall, focused attempt, feedback, transfer, and return.
- Public course structure and source state can be inspected before account creation.
- Source-backed, model-knowledge, hybrid, and legacy states remain distinct.
- Self-report, observed practice, and assessed evidence remain separate.
- Course creation accepts a specific language or bilingual pairing and validates generated content against that request.
- Review and evidence remain connected to the course instead of becoming separate tools.

**Why that is better:** The learner sees a bounded path, practices the actual capability, returns to a useful next action, and can inspect both demonstrated and unresolved evidence.

## Competitive Landscape

**Direct:** AI-assisted course builders and self-directed learning workspaces — often emphasize generation volume without Filosage's evidence and source-state separation.
**Secondary:** Course marketplaces, tutoring products, tutorial libraries, and generic chat — solve parts of the job but leave the learner to connect goal, practice, review, and proof.
**Indirect:** Improvised notes, standalone flashcards, search tabs, and postponing the goal.

Named competitor claims require current primary-source research before publication. This context records alternative categories, not unverified competitor comparisons.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| “Is the AI always right?” | No. Filosage separates verified sources, disclosed model knowledge, optional reading, and legacy references; consequential claims still require current authoritative verification. |
| “Does the evidence prove mastery?” | No. It records self-report, observed practice, and assessed criteria. It is not a credential, verified identity claim, or guarantee of mastery. |
| “Is the app localized?” | The interface is English today. Eligible course creation supports a requested course language or bilingual pairing; that is course-content support, not full interface localization or a native-speaker guarantee. |
| “Can my school or team administer learners?” | No. Filosage is currently an individual learning product, not a school, classroom, team, or enterprise LMS. |
| “Are paid memberships open?” | Only when the Plans page and billing status explicitly say checkout is open. A pricing link, waitlist entry, or saved preference does not create a charge or subscription. |

**Anti-personas:** Under-13 users; schools or teams needing administration; people seeking accreditation or verified credentials; people expecting autonomous high-stakes medical, legal, or financial advice; anyone requiring guaranteed outcomes, fully localized UI, unlimited AI, or unreviewed public publishing.

## Switching Dynamics

**Push:** Disconnected resources, unclear sequencing, passive consumption, forgotten material, and progress records that confuse completion with capability.
**Pull:** One goal, a focused course, deliberate practice, useful return timing, visible source status, and inspectable evidence.
**Habit:** Familiar search, videos, generic chat, existing marketplace accounts, and personal notes.
**Anxiety:** AI reliability, source quality, privacy, time commitment, course quality, language quality, paid availability, and whether the record means what it appears to mean.

## Customer Language

No completed customer-language dataset exists yet. The following is owner-approved product language, not a customer quote:

- “Turn a goal you care about into a course you can practice.”
- “See what the evidence says—and what it does not.”
- “Start a search you can edit.”
- “Portable evidence,” not “professional evidence.”

**Words to use:** goal, focused course, deliberate practice, useful next step, requested course language, source status, observed practice, assessed criteria, portable evidence, pending, revocable.
**Words to avoid:** learn anything, guaranteed mastery, personalized for people like you, professional-only, English learner, fully localized, credential, certified, unlimited, human-reviewed unless literally true, proven demand, best course for you.

## Brand Voice

**Tone:** Calm, curious, precise, honest, and quietly premium.
**Style:** Outcome first; plain language; specific capability and limitation; no synthetic urgency or generic AI-platform language.
**Personality:** Considered, capable, learner-controlled, source-aware, humane.

## Capability Availability and Claim Register

| Capability or claim | Status | Public wording rule |
|---|---|---|
| Inspect published outcomes, modules, lesson titles, assessments, and source state | Implemented | May be stated directly. |
| Open published lesson bodies and save progress | Implemented for verified accounts | Always state the account boundary. |
| Notes, practice, review scheduling, tutor allowance, and learning evidence | Implemented with account and applicable quota | May be stated with account/quota conditions. |
| Private AI-assisted course creation | Implemented but entitlement, credit, provider, safety, and quality gated | Describe as Plus/Pro/owner capability and never promise generation success. |
| Requested non-English or bilingual course content | Implemented in Course Studio and pipeline validation | State English interface separately; do not claim full localization or native-speaker quality. |
| Flashcard decks and AI generation | Implemented but runtime-flag gated | Do not list as currently available on public plans until hosted flags are verified. |
| V2 labs, lesson visuals, validation, repair, and advanced pipeline surfaces | Implemented behind owner/cohort/runtime flags | Do not market as generally available. |
| Portable evidence export, revocable share links, and cross-attempt analysis | Implemented for Pro/owner entitlements | State that on-screen evidence remains available and that exports are not credentials. |
| Course publishing | Implemented for Pro/owner after completion/review conditions | Do not imply automatic or unreviewed publication. |
| Paid checkout | Implemented behind billing lock | State current published status; keep closed while `BILLING_ENABLED=false`. |
| Email, push, SMS, or automatic calendar delivery | Not offered | Do not claim. Calendar files are generated on-device only. |
| Customer demand, efficacy, employment outcomes, testimonials, credentials | Unvalidated or unsupported | Prohibited until independent evidence exists. |

## Proof Points

**Repository-supported proof:** Complete public course outlines; visible source-state labels; content-language validation; practice and review records; self-report/observed/assessed evidence separation; entitlement-aware private creation; Pro export/share and publishing code paths.

**Local verification:** Build and automated acceptance evidence may prove the current branch behavior only. It must not be presented as hosted availability, customer demand, or learning efficacy.

**Customers, testimonials, and outcome metrics:** None approved for marketing use. Do not create or imply them.

## Message Hierarchy

1. Turn a meaningful goal into a focused course and deliberate practice.
2. Return to a useful next step and inspect what the evidence supports.
3. See source state, privacy boundaries, and requested course-language support clearly.
4. Explain AI assistance, quotas, flags, and availability as implementation conditions—not the headline.

## Tier Story

- **Free:** Learn from published courses and keep available practice, notes, progress, review, and evidence connected.
- **Plus:** Create private courses around goals the published library does not cover, subject to course credits and current availability.
- **Pro:** Add advanced portable evidence, revocable sharing, cross-attempt analysis, and publishing under current completion/review rules.

## Goals and Measurement Contract

**Business goal:** Find a repeatable audience, activation path, return behavior, and sustainable offer without weakening learning, privacy, or billing controls.
**Primary conversion action:** A qualified learner opens a public course and reaches first completed practice.
**Acquisition:** Anonymous consented session sequence from landing view to course discovery to signup start; never presented as completed-account conversion.
**Activation:** Verified account sequence from signup completion through course start, first completed practice, demonstrated criterion, and evidence report view.
**Retention:** Day 7/28 return after first completed practice using only meaningful verified learning actions.
**Paid interest:** Explicit server-recorded preference, waitlist, or checkout action; a pricing query alone is not intent.
**Research status:** Repository support does not equal market validation. Market claims require completed participant or behavioral evidence recorded in the hypothesis register.
