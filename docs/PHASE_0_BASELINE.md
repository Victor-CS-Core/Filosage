## Why values are pending

The professional-outcome position, unique actor tracking, acquisition attribution, and outcome funnel began in this phase. Historical traffic cannot be transformed into reliable values for events that were not previously collected.

Zero in the owner dashboard means “none observed in the selected window,” not “the product has disproven demand.” Values must come from non-owner production traffic and real participants.

## Existing operational baseline

The repository already contains:

- Public anonymous course access.
- Account progress and review scheduling.
- Lesson retrieval, transfer tasks, and capstones.
- Server-authorized private creation and owner publishing.
- AI request quotas, budget pools, idempotency, and cost attribution.
- Safety and account administration.
- Stripe-compatible billing surfaces held behind a disabled activation lock.
- Automated accessibility, authorization, learning-flow, and cost tests.

These are readiness capabilities, not proof of product-market fit.

## Phase 0 gate

Proceed to Phase 1 only when:

- 15–20 qualified interviews are complete.
- At least ten participants describe the problem without prompting.
- At least five commit to a structured learning outcome in the current product.
- One acquisition channel can recruit qualified participants repeatedly.
- The dashboard can measure landing, course start, lesson start, first practice, and demonstrated evidence.
- The flagship curriculum and Mastery Graph design have been reviewed.

## Data-quality checks

Review weekly:

- Known owner traffic remains excluded.
- No private notes, tutor prompts, learner goals, or capstone text appears in analytics.
- Funnel unique actors use stable first-party random IDs.
- Acquisition stores only broad campaign labels, hostname, and landing path.
- Duplicate sessions do not inflate one-time events.
- Test and local traffic are not interpreted as market demand.

## First decision report

Complete after the interview and traffic thresholds:

- Strongest recurring trigger.
- Best-performing role group.
- Most credible existing substitute.
- Largest trust objection.
- Percentage committing calendar time.
- Landing-to-course conversion.
- Qualification rate.
- Recommended decision: continue, narrow, pivot, or stop.
