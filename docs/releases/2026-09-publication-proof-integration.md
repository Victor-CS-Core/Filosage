# Publication evidence and account-session integration

The course studio now offers **Validate draft** for private managed courses. An owner decision submits the exact displayed proof token. A stale-token response discards the old reason and source selections; validation and a new decision are required even when the content hash did not change. Course reads, validation, publication and review responses are scoped to the current account/session generation, and account changes abort earlier requests.

Publication proof policy v2 additionally binds retained automatic source evidence to its stored research artifact, author, request fingerprint, provider response, source pack and current research policy. Its expiration is the earliest applicable artifact/source expiration, capped by the research policy (30 days normally; 24 hours for current-claims requirements). These are reuse limits, not guarantees that claims remain accurate. Validation and publication transactions reread the bound artifact; expired or substituted evidence cannot publish. Further-reading changes do not invalidate independent claim evidence.

Current-claims drafts require fresh traceable research, including legacy drafts. The current recovery action is to create a new draft with current research and retain the existing draft until the replacement is ready; this change does not invent an in-place paid research-refresh endpoint. Registered diagnostic CQ_SOURCE_006 is non-overridable. Legacy manually supplied sources outside this current-claims requirement retain their existing explicit review contract.

Independent review caught an account-switch race and an unregistered diagnostic; both were corrected. Local checks: 39 actual file-store/publication-route behavior cases, 18 focused source/publication contracts, TypeScript and focused lint passed. Two browser smoke regressions are registered and collect successfully; browser execution remains a CI gate. Provider moderation, factual accuracy, hosted identity and production publication are not established by these fixtures. The two overlapping deployment revisions must both enforce the publication/account write protocol before a safe traffic swap or rollback.
