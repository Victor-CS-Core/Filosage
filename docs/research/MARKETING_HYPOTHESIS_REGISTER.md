# Filosage Marketing Hypothesis Register

**Created:** 2026-08-16
**Owner decision:** All four learning situations are valid product entry points. None is market-validated by that decision or by repository capability alone.

## Status vocabulary

- **Repository-supported:** Current product contracts and code can serve the declared situation without adding a new audience-specific feature.
- **Repository-unsupported:** A required product capability is absent or blocked.
- **Market-unvalidated:** No qualified participant or behavioral evidence establishes demand.
- **Directional:** Early evidence exists but has not passed the stated gate.
- **Validated:** The stated participant and behavior gate has been met and recorded.

## Claims gate

Before broad promotion, the existing Phase 0 gate still requires:

- 15–20 qualified interviews in the selected first campaign cohort;
- at least ten unprompted descriptions of the target problem;
- at least five commitments to complete a structured learning outcome;
- one repeatable acquisition channel worth testing;
- qualified course starts, first completed practice, and meaningful return measured without joining anonymous and account identities into one person-level funnel.

Until those conditions are recorded, the four buttons are browsing entry points—not personalization, recommendations, validated segments, or proof of demand.
